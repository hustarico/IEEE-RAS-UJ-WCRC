About the Challenge

Participants are tasked with designing and building a robot capable of climbing a vertical wall against gravity. This isn't just about sticking to a surface; it's about navigating upwards with precision. Competitors will be evaluated on speed, stability, and the successful completion of the climb. This is a true test of engineering precision, mechanical design, and innovation.
